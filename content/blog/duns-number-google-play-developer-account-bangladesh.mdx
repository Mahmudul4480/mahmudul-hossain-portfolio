I'm launching **Salah Upgrader**, a prayer-reminder app, on Google Play. I thought the hard part would be the Flutter code. It wasn't. The hard part was a nine-digit number called D-U-N-S.

This is not a "what is D-U-N-S" article. This is what happened to me in the first week, in order, with the mistakes included. If you're a Bangladeshi developer about to open a Play Console **organization** account, this might save you money and a few nights' sleep. Here's what getting a **D-U-N-S number for Google Play developer account** verification from Bangladesh really looked like.

## Personal or organization? I chose the slow road

Google gives you two choices:

- **Personal account:** no D-U-N-S, but before going to production you need a closed test with at least **12 testers for 14 days**.
- **Organization account:** needs a D-U-N-S number and company documents, but no closed-test requirement, and the store shows a verified organisation.

I had no rush. My exact words to my assistant were: *"aste dhirei hok, company diye korbo."* Slowly is fine, I'll do it as a company.

## Problem 1: My trade licence was in Bangla and expired

The D&B form wants your legal name in English. My licence is in Bangla. Fix: transliterate the name and keep **exactly the same spelling** on D&B, Play Console, Google Payments profile and the bank. Google checks that the name and address match across all of them.

The bigger issue: the licence had expired. Renewal on the city-corporation portal showed overdue fees plus a late surcharge, and moving it to an IT/software category would cost even more. In total it was around 30,000 Taka I couldn't spend right now.

**Lesson:** never "fix" a date on a PDF. Google says modified documents can get your account and apps removed. Renew it properly or use another legal entity.

## Problem 2: Switching to a company that was already up to date

I'm a director in another registered limited company whose papers were all current. A Play organization account doesn't have to match your app's category (a non-tech company can publish a prayer app). Google checks that the company is real and that the details match.

What I gathered:
- Renewed trade licence
- TIN and BIN certificates
- Certificate of Incorporation and Form XII (list of directors)
- Memorandum & Articles of Association
- A written authorisation from the MD, since the licence was in his name and I'm the contact person

Small catch: the postcode on the licence didn't match the tax certificates. Check these before you submit, not after.

## Problem 3: The $650 phone call

I applied through **dnbsame.com**, D&B's official South Asia partner site (dnb.com redirects there for Bangladesh). The next day someone called claiming to be from "DUNS", asking for a one-time **$650** and wanting to meet in person to "finalise".

Red flags:
- Google says applying for D-U-N-S through Dun & Bradstreet is **completely free**
- The caller name came from a caller-ID app where anyone can set any name
- The number wasn't on D&B Bangladesh's official contact page
- No email, no reference, just "let's meet"

I didn't pay and didn't meet. I sent one SMS asking for everything in writing from an official D&B email, and separately emailed the official address on their contact page.

## What the real D&B asked for

Through the official channel I got a written reply from the **dnbsame.com** domain. It said the number is **free of cost** and takes **at least 30 working days**. They asked for:

1. Trade licence
2. TIN and BIN certificates
3. Certificate of Incorporation
4. Company profile (if available)
5. Their filled-in D-U-N-S application form
6. **Audited financial report**

I didn't have this year's audited report yet, so I sent everything else and asked if last year's would do. (Tip: Gmail limits how many files you can attach comfortably, so I merged related PDFs into one.)

## Where it stands

As I write this (November 4, 2026), I'm still waiting. D&B says it can take up to 30 working days, and I'll add the ending here once the number arrives. The app is ready to prepare for launch, but the launch date and "download now" ads are on hold until the number arrives.

## My checklist if you're doing this from Bangladesh

- Decide first: personal (12 testers × 14 days) or organization (D-U-N-S, up to 30 working days)
- Renew the trade licence **before** you apply
- Same English spelling of name and address everywhere
- Keep incorporation certificate, Form XII, MoA/AoA, TIN/BIN, an audited report and a short company profile ready in one folder
- Apply via the official site; expect follow-up by email
- **Never pay for a D-U-N-S number for Google Play.** Anything that asks for money or an in-person meeting: get it in writing from an official domain first
- Don't edit documents. Ever.
