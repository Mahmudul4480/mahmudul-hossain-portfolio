# Salah Upgrader

Static landing page for the free Salah Upgrader prayer-reminder app by Mahmudul Hossain
(package `com.mahmudulhossain.salahupgrader`).

Served at `/salah-upgrader` and `/salah-upgrader/`. `index.html` plus `assets/` are the page.
The privacy policy lives at `/salah-upgrader/privacy` (Next.js App Router page).

Donate and waitlist controls on the landing are placeholders — leave them as they are until
those flows are wired up.
