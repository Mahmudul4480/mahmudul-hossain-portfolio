# Chattogram artwork

The illustrations and motion on `/services/web-developer-chittagong` are ported from
Mahmudul Hossain's own page at socialmediacaring.com/chittagong
(`Mahmudul4480/social-media-caring`, `src/components/ctg/` and `src/styles/ctg.css`).

Skyline, Karnaphuli bridge, port cranes, container ships, Patenga shoreline, CRB
rain-trees, mezban deg, and industry icons are original SVGs drawn for that page.
Colours on this site are remapped to the portfolio navy, cyan, and amber tokens.
The Social Media Care logo and name are not used here.

## Clips

- `public/ctg/clips/port.*` and `bridge.*`: the same muted loops published with that
  page (AI-generated with Higgsfield on the free tier, supplied by the owner).
  Patenga and CRB still use the original SVG fallbacks.
- Videos stay on their poster until the frame is near the viewport.
